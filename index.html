ABOUT US

At BoundlessBox Studio, we're all about moulding and breaking the limits of one's imagination. As a passionate game development studio, we dedicate our heart to craft unforgettable experiences that captures the golden era of gaming, where fun and creativity have no bounds.

ABOUT TEAM

Amit Kharb, known online as *carbontedLemonJuice*, is the sole developer behind BoundlessBox Studio. With a deep passion for computers and video games from a young age, Amit is dedicated to creating games that are both enjoyable and memorable. Having participated in numerous game jams, both solo and as part of teams, he has gained valuable experience in game development. Currently, Amit is working on two exciting projects under the BoundlessBox Studio brand.
