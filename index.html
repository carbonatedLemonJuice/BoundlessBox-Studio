ABOUT US

At BoundlessBox Studio, we're all about moulding and breaking the limits of one's imagination. As a passionate game development studio, we dedicate our heart to craft unforgettable experiences that captures the golden era of gaming, where fun and creativity have no bounds. We are based in Delhi, India. Our team is a group of young, talented and passionate individuals with love for gaming and game development. We are currently working on our upcoming title, Monsters in the Dark.

ABOUT TEAM

Amit Kharb, known online as *carbontedLemonJuice*, is the Founder of BoundlessBox Studio. He is programmer, director, designer and musician. With a deep passion for computers and video games from a young age, Amit is dedicated to creating games that are both enjoyable and memorable. Having participated in numerous game jams, both solo and as part of teams, he has gained valuable experience in game development. Currently, Amit is directing Monsters in the Dark game.

Aditya Shukla, known online as “…….” , oh wait the guy has no online presence, is an aspiring game designer. Ever since Aditya was a child, he was infatuated with video games and wanted to explore more about the world of games. This passion of games led him to the amazing world of game design. Though having limited experience, Aditya is determined to change the Indian Gaming Industry for good; with an aim to transform the industry it into the most interesting, diverse and fun industry in the entire cosmos. Currently Aditya is collaborating with Amit in the upcoming title- “Monsters in the Dark”

Abheesht Kodati is a name that stands on its own, needing no alias to make an impact. From an early age, he was drawn to video games—not just for the thrill but for the deep, immersive stories they told. To him, games were more than entertainment; they were new realities to explore. It wasn’t just about playing but experiencing and living the narratives. In these worlds, his name found its place, resonating with the essence of storytelling and adventure.
